help Frequently Asked Questions
Is this really free? ▼
Yes! Browser AI uses Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano model. No API keys, no costs, no usage limits.
How is this different from ChatGPT? ▼
ChatGPT runs on OpenAI's servers (requires internet/API key). Browser AI runs entirely in your browser (no server, no API key, works offline after setup).
Is my data safe? ▼
Absolutely! All AI processing happens locally in your browser. Your financial data never leaves your device. No data is sent to any server, including Google or Microsoft.
Which browsers are supported? ▼
Currently supported: Chrome 127+, Chrome Canary, Edge 127+ (Chromium-based). Coming soon: Firefox and Safari.
