UFIN · Free · Open Source

Ukrainian Financial Statement Analysis Tool

A free, browser-based calculator for Ukrainian balance sheets (Form 1) and income statements (Form 2). Compute liquidity, solvency, profitability, financial-stability and business-activity ratios across two reporting years — with interactive charts and an optional on-device AI assistant (Chrome Gemini Nano). No registration, no server-side processing, works offline as a Progressive Web App.

What you can analyze

  • Liquidity ratios

    Current, quick, cash and absolute liquidity ratios; asset-liquidity grouping from the balance sheet.

  • Solvency & financial stability

    Debt-to-equity, autonomy coefficient, financial independence, solvency recovery and the three-component stability indicator.

  • Profitability (ROA, ROE)

    Return on assets, return on equity, net and gross margins, profitability of capital and fixed assets.

  • Business activity & turnover

    Asset, receivables, inventory and payables turnover; operating-cycle duration; capital turnover.

  • Working capital & fixed assets

    Working-capital efficiency, fixed-asset wear and renewal coefficients, factor analysis of productivity.

  • AI financial assistant

    Optional on-device Gemini Nano assistant (Chrome 127+). Your financial data never leaves the browser.
